You = customer operates the layer. Provider = provider operates it. Shared = provider manages the storage/platform while you remain accountable for your data, access, and governance.

Chart improvements: PaaS generally includes a provider-managed runtime. SaaS providers operate the application, too. Even with SaaS, customers still manage users, access settings, configuration, and data governance.

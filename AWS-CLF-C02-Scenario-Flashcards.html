You = customer operates the layer. Provider = provider operates it. Shared = provider manages the storage/platform while you remain accountable for your data, access, and governance.

Chart improvements: PaaS generally includes a provider-managed runtime. SaaS providers operate the application, too. Even with SaaS, customers still manage users, access settings, configuration, and data governance. For Elastic Beanstalk and Lightsail, review the actual service responsibilities rather than relying only on a category label.
